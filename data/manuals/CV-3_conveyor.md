# Belt Conveyor CV-3 — Operator and Maintenance Manual (sample)

Machine type: conveyor. Units on the floor: L2-CONV-01 (packing line), L2-CONV-02 (dispatch).
This is a fictional sample manual written for the FloorMitra demo.

## 1. Safety
- Never reach under or into a moving belt. Use the pull-cord emergency stop along the side.
- For belt tracking, roller change or cleaning under the belt: follow SOP-LOTO-01.

## 2. Common problems
| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Belt moving to one side (mistracking) | Tail pulley out of line, dirt on rollers | Lockout. Clean the rollers. Adjust the tail pulley take-up bolt a quarter turn on the side the belt moves toward, then run empty and watch for 2 minutes. |
| Belt slipping, motor running | Belt too loose or wet/oily belt | Lockout. Tighten the take-up evenly on both sides. Clean oil from the belt. |
| Squealing noise from one roller | Roller bearing worn | Mark the roller. Raise a fault ticket: replace roller assembly RL-50. |
| Conveyor stops, fault light C-3 | Motor overload / jammed product | Clear the jam after lockout. Reset once. If it trips again, raise a ticket. |
| Fault light C-7 | Pull-cord pressed somewhere on the line | Walk the line, find the pulled cord, reset it, then restart. |

## 3. Daily checks (operator)
- Walk the line: no torn belt edge, no product stuck under the belt, all pull-cords reset.
- Listen for squealing rollers.
