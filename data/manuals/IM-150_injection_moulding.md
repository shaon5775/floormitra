# Injection Moulding Machine IM-150 — Operator and Maintenance Manual (sample)

Machine type: injection_moulding. Unit on the floor: L3-IMM-01.
This is a fictional sample manual written for the FloorMitra demo.

## 1. Safety
- Never put a hand inside the mould area unless the safety gate is open and the pump is OFF.
- Heater bands are at 180–260 °C. Use heat-proof gloves when purging.
- Work on the hydraulics or heaters: follow SOP-LOTO-01.

## 2. Alarm codes
| Code | Meaning | What the operator does |
|---|---|---|
| H-11 | Barrel heater zone 2 not heating | Check the zone 2 temperature on the screen. If it stays cold, the heater band HB-150 or its fuse has failed. Raise a fault ticket. |
| H-14 | Oil temperature high (above 55 °C) | Check that cooling water is flowing (valve open, flow 20 L/min). Clean the oil cooler strainer. |
| H-20 | Mould not closing fully | Check for a stuck part or flash in the mould. Clean the parting line. Do not increase clamp force on your own. |
| H-31 | Safety gate switch fault | Do not bypass the gate switch. Raise a fault ticket (part GS-01). |

## 3. Part quality problems
- Short shots (part not filled): raise injection pressure by 5 bar steps, check that the hopper is not empty.
- Flash (extra thin plastic at the edges): lower injection pressure, check the mould for damage.
- Black specks: purge the barrel. If it continues, the screw or barrel may need cleaning (maintenance).
