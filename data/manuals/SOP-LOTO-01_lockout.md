# SOP-LOTO-01 — Lockout / Tagout (sample plant SOP)

Applies to every machine on the floor. This is a fictional sample SOP written for the FloorMitra demo.

## 1. When lockout is required
- Any work where a hand, tool or body part goes inside a guard, belt, roller, mould or panel.
- Cleaning jams, changing belts, rollers, filters on running equipment, electrical work.

## 2. Steps
1. Tell the shift supervisor and the operators nearby.
2. Stop the machine with the normal stop button.
3. Switch OFF the main isolator. Release stored energy: air (bleed to 0 bar), hydraulics (pressure 0), springs.
4. Put YOUR OWN padlock and a signed tag on the isolator. One person = one lock.
5. Try to start the machine. It must NOT start. Put the switch back to OFF.
6. Do the work.
7. Remove tools, put guards back, tell people nearby, then remove your own lock and tag.

## 3. Never
- Never remove another person's lock.
- Never bypass a safety gate or guard switch.
