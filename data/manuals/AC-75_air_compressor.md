# Screw Air Compressor AC-75 — Operator and Maintenance Manual (sample)

Machine type: air_compressor. Unit on the floor: UT-COMP-01 (utility room, feeds all lines).
This is a fictional sample manual written for the FloorMitra demo.

## 1. Safety
- The compressor can start by itself (auto restart). Before opening any panel, follow SOP-LOTO-01 and
  release the air pressure: the tank gauge must show 0 bar.
- Hot surfaces: the oil separator and discharge pipe can be above 80 °C. Wait 20 minutes after stopping.

## 2. Alarm codes
| Code | Meaning | What the operator does |
|---|---|---|
| A-01 | High discharge temperature (above 105 °C) | Check the room ventilation and that the cooler fins are clean. Check the oil level. If it repeats, raise a fault ticket (oil filter OF-75 or oil may be due). |
| A-02 | Low oil level | Stop the compressor. Top up only with the same compressor oil (ISO VG 46). Look for leaks under the unit. |
| A-05 | Air filter blocked (pressure drop high) | Replace the intake air filter, part AIF-75. Do not run without a filter. |
| A-09 | Motor overload trip | Do not reset more than once. Raise a fault ticket for maintenance (electrical check). |
| A-12 | Pressure not reaching 7 bar | Check for air leaks on the lines (hissing sound). Check the intake filter. If the leak is on the unit, raise a ticket. |

## 3. Air pressure for the lines
- Normal working pressure: 7.0 bar at the compressor, at least 5 bar at every machine.
- If one line has low pressure but the compressor shows 7 bar, the problem is on that line (leak or blocked
  filter-regulator), not the compressor.

## 4. Maintenance schedule
- Daily: drain condensate from the tank (open the drain valve for 5 seconds).
- Every 500 hours: clean the cooler fins, check the intake air filter AIF-75.
- Every 2,000 hours: change oil (ISO VG 46, 12 litres), oil filter OF-75 and separator element SE-75.

## 5. Spare parts
| Part no. | Name |
|---|---|
| AIF-75 | Intake air filter |
| OF-75 | Oil filter |
| SE-75 | Oil separator element |
| OIL-46 | Compressor oil ISO VG 46, 20 L can |
